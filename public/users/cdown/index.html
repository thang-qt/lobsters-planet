Debunking zswap and zram myths

tl;dr: If in doubt, prefer to use zswap. Only use zram if you have a highly specific reason to. In terms of architecture: zswap sits in front of your disk swap, compresses pages in RAM, and automatically tiers cold data to disk. It integrates directly with the kernel's memory management and distributes pressure gracefully. zram is a compressed RAM block device with a hard capacity limit.
